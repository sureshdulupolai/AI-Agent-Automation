Standalone Embed Test

OmniBot Universal Embed Demonstration

This demo page simulates an external business website (e.g. Shopify, Webflow, or custom React site) embedding the OmniBot widget.

Embedded Script Tag

Look at the bottom right corner of your screen! The floating AI chatbot bubble is loaded dynamically via:

<script src="/widget.js" data-bot-id="bot-apex-agency"></script>

Interactive Verification Steps

1. Click the floating chat bubble on the bottom right.

2. Ask questions such as: What services do you offer? or How much does a custom SaaS cost?

3. Drop your name and email/phone to test the automatic lead extraction engine!
